# Marketing Attribution & ROAS Optimization

## 📊 Project Overview

End-to-end data analytics project implementing **Multi-Touch Attribution modeling** to optimize marketing budget allocation. Analyzes 10,000 customers, 1,500+ conversions, and ₹30+ Lakhs in marketing spend across 6 channels.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Most companies use Last-Touch Attribution, which overvalues conversion channels by 81% and leads to 30-40% marketing budget waste.

## 💡 Solution

Built comprehensive attribution framework comparing 6 models, with **Markov Chain** as the recommended data-driven approach.

## 🔑 Key Findings

- Email overvalued by 81% using Last-Touch (₹12.27L vs reality ₹6.79L)
- Paid Search drives 40% of customer journeys
- Social channels critical for mid-funnel (invisible in Last-Touch)
- Converted customers need 5.54 touchpoints on average

## 📈 Business Impact

- **Revenue Lift**: +₹3.06 Lakhs (+11.4%) with same budget
- **ROAS Improvement**: 11.4% increase
- **Recommendation**: Increase Email budget 80%, maintain acquisition channels

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **Analysis**: Python (pandas, scikit-learn, statsmodels)
- **Visualization**: Power BI, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Models**: Markov Chain, Time-Decay, Position-Based Attribution
